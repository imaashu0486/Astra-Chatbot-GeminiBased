Artificial Intelligence Overview

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science.
2. It focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence.
3. Types of AI include:
   - Narrow AI
   - General AI
   - Super AI
4. AI is used in applications like:
   - Chatbots
   - Self-driving cars
   - Recommendation systems
5. The performance of AI depends heavily on data quality and algorithms.

Conclusion:
AI is transforming industries by automating tasks and improving decision-making.
